Ginny Delivers

Ginny Delivers is the private production tool of the YouTube channel Ginny Delivers, run by Anastasiia Klymenko. It helps her plan, write and produce her own videos.

The tool connects to YouTube with read-only access, and only to her own channel. It reads her channel's public video list and its YouTube Analytics reports (views, watch time, audience retention, impressions, traffic sources), so she can see how her videos perform and plan the next ones. It never uploads, edits or deletes anything, and it does not read anyone else's channel.

The tool is not offered to the public. Nobody else signs in to it.
