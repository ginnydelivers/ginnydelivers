Privacy Policy

Ginny Delivers is the private production tool of the YouTube channel Ginny Delivers, run by Anastasiia Klymenko. This page explains what the tool reads from Google and what happens to it. Effective 20 November 2026.

Кратко: инструмент только читает данные её собственного YouTube-канала, хранит их у неё и никому не передаёт и не продаёт.

What it reads

When the channel owner signs in with Google, the tool asks for two read-only permissions:

  • youtube.readonly: the list of her channel's videos and their public details (title, publish date, duration).
  • yt-analytics.readonly: her channel's YouTube Analytics and Reporting data (views, watch time, audience retention, impressions, click-through rate, traffic sources).

It does not ask for permission to upload, edit or delete anything, and it does not read email, contacts, files or any other Google data.

What it does with it

It turns the figures into a short performance table that she uses to plan and improve her next videos. Nothing else.

Where it is stored

The sign-in token and the downloaded data are stored on her own computer and in her own private GitHub repository. The people she engages to build and maintain the tool may handle this data on her behalf, only for that purpose.

Sharing

The data is never sold, never shared with third parties, never used for advertising and never used to train AI models. The use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

How to revoke access

Remove "Ginny Delivers" at myaccount.google.com/permissions. The tool then can no longer read anything. To have the stored data deleted, write to the contact below.
